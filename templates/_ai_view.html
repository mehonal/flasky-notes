Enable Vault Context for this conversation?

When enabled, each message you send triggers a private, on-device search of your notes. The most relevant notes (titles + contents) are decrypted and included as context for the AI to read.

Your Flasky server never sees this plaintext — it stays end-to-end encrypted. But your AI provider (Ollama) will receive and read the relevant note contents.

Before each message where notes are actually shared, you'll see exactly which notes will be sent and can choose to share them, send without context, or cancel.

This applies to this conversation only. You can turn it off at any time.
