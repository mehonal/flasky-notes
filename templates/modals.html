Enable AI Web Search for this chat?

When enabled, the AI may search the web and fetch web pages to answer your questions with up-to-date information.

Proceed with caution. The AI generates its own search queries — including rephrasing your question — and these leave your device, along with the contents of any pages it fetches. Think about what your question reveals before enabling this.

Your search provider (Ollama) is responsible for how it handles web searches and fetched pages — how queries and page contents are processed, retained, logged, or shared is governed by their policies, not by Flasky. Review your provider's privacy policy before enabling.

Your Flasky server stores none of it — queries and fetched page contents pass through your server unencrypted and are not persisted. Only your message and the final answer are stored, end-to-end encrypted as usual.

You can turn it off at any time.
